## What you will make

Design and code a game where the player must collect objects, making sure to avoid the monster!

Click **Run** in the editor below to try the finished game. Then type commands into the **Text output**, such as `go north` to move between rooms and `get key` to collect items, and press **Enter** after each one. Avoid the monster!
